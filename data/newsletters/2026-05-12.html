Microsoft Copilot Wave 3 & Agent 365 Launch

If your business runs on Microsoft 365 — and the odds are good that it does — May 1, 2026 was a bigger day than you might realize. Microsoft officially launched Copilot Wave 3 and its new Agent 365 platform, bringing multi-agent AI orchestration directly into Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams. This is the most significant Microsoft AI upgrade for everyday business users yet, and the best part? You likely already have access through your existing subscription.

Here's what's new:

  • Multi-model orchestration is the headline feature. Agent 365 and Copilot Wave 3 (both launched May 1, 2026) automatically route your tasks to Claude, GPT, or Microsoft's own models — whichever handles that type of job best. You don't have to choose or switch. (Source)
  • Excel got a serious upgrade. The Python integration is now paired with cross-workbook analysis, letting SMBs run advanced data work — forecasting, trend analysis, multi-sheet comparisons — without needing a data science team. (Source)
  • Teams automation is here. Copilot Wave 3 includes deeper Teams integration with multi-agent workflows, so small teams can automate meeting follow-ups, project tracking, and client communication — running in the background while you focus on the actual work. (Source)

Business Impact

SMBs already paying for Microsoft 365 subscriptions now have enterprise-grade AI automation built directly into Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams — with no new software to buy, no new logins to manage, and no steep learning curve.

Your Takeaway This Week
