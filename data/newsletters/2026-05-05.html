Cloud & Infrastructure

OpenAI Goes Multi-Cloud: GPT-5.5 Hits Amazon Bedrock

For years, if you wanted OpenAI's best models, you either went through OpenAI directly or through Microsoft Azure — full stop. That just changed in a big way. OpenAI and Microsoft renegotiated their landmark partnership this week, ending exclusivity so OpenAI can now distribute models — including the brand-new GPT-5.5 — through Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud. This is one of the most significant structural shifts in AI distribution we've seen, and it has real, immediate implications for your business.

What you need to know:

  • Microsoft and OpenAI ended their exclusive cloud arrangement, opening the door for GPT-5.5 and future OpenAI models to be distributed via Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud.
  • GPT-5.5 is now live for AWS customers through Bedrock — with your existing AWS security controls, billing, and service-level agreements applying directly out of the box.
  • Multi-provider AI strategies are now a real option: businesses can route workloads to the cheapest or fastest provider, with automatic failover if one service goes down.

Business Impact

SMBs already on AWS or Google Cloud can now access OpenAI's most capable models directly within their existing cloud accounts — no new vendor contracts, billing relationships, or security reviews required.

Your move this week:

If your business is already on AWS, log into the Amazon Bedrock console and check whether GPT-5.5 is available in your region. Experiment with one current use case — a customer email draft, a product description, a data summary — and compare results against what you're already using. The goal isn't to switch everything overnight; it's to understand your options so you're not locked in.

Finance & Operations

AI Financial Agents Are Replacing SMB Back-Office Staff

Here's a stat worth pausing on: in 2023, 36% of U.S. small businesses were investing in AI. Today, in 2026, that number is 57% — and financial automation is the category leading that charge. A new wave of AI financial agents purpose-built for SMBs launched this week, automating accounts receivable, cash flow forecasting, and real-time financial monitoring at price points that would have seemed impossible just two years ago. Starting at $20/month per agent, these tools put CFO-level financial intelligence within reach of businesses with as few as five employees.

What you need to know:

  • 57% of U.S. small businesses are now actively investing in AI — up sharply from 36% in 2023 — with financial automation leading all other adoption categories.
  • These AI agents monitor key financial metrics, automate receivables, and predict cash flow gaps before they become crises — capabilities that previously required dedicated CFO or controller staff.
  • AI agents for SMBs are now accessible starting from $20/month per agent, with lead follow-up and financial workflows consistently delivering the highest measured ROI.

Business Impact

Small businesses can now deploy AI agents to handle invoicing, cash flow monitoring, and financial forecasting at a fraction of the cost of a part-time bookkeeper — freeing owners to focus on growth instead of chasing numbers.

Your move this week:

Think about the one financial task that eats the most time on your team — whether that's chasing late invoices, building weekly cash flow reports, or manually reconciling accounts. Search for an AI financial agent that integrates with your existing accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks all have growing ecosystems here) and sign up for a free trial. Even a 30-day pilot on one workflow will tell you whether the ROI is real for your situation.

Productivity & Tools

Microsoft Copilot Wave 3 & Agent 365 Launch for Business

If your business runs on Microsoft 365 — and a lot of you do — May 1st was a big day. Microsoft officially launched Copilot Wave 3 and the new Agent 365 platform, bringing multi-model AI orchestration directly into the apps your team uses every single day: Teams, Outlook, Excel, and Word. This isn't a feature update. This is the most ambitious Microsoft 365 AI upgrade since Copilot originally launched, and it's available right now inside the subscription you're already paying for.

What you need to know:

  • Agent 365 and Copilot Wave 3 launched May 1, 2026, featuring multi-model orchestration that automatically routes your tasks to Claude, GPT-5.5, or Microsoft's own models — whichever produces the best result for that specific job.
  • Excel now includes AI-powered Python integration with cross-workbook analysis — meaning non-technical business owners can run sophisticated data analysis across multiple spreadsheets without writing a single line of code.
  • With 80% of enterprise apps expected to embed AI agents by end of 2026, Microsoft 365's 400 million+ business users are now sitting at the center of the most significant productivity shift in decades.

Business Impact

SMBs already paying for Microsoft 365 subscriptions get powerful AI automation across every app they use daily — no additional tools, no new logins, and no developer needed to get started.

Your move this week:

Open Microsoft 365 and check whether the Copilot icon has updated in your Outlook or Teams sidebar — the Wave 3 rollout is staged, so availability may vary by plan. If you have access, try the new cross-workbook Excel analysis first: pull two reports you'd normally combine manually, describe what you want in plain English, and let the AI do the merge. If Wave 3 hasn't reached your account yet, now is the time to confirm your Microsoft 365 plan includes Copilot — it may require an add-on, and the sooner you activate it, the sooner you get the upgrade.
