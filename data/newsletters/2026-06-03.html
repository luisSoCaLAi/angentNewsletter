Productivity & Tools

ChatGPT Lands Inside Excel & Google Sheets

OpenAI just rolled out ChatGPT natively inside Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets as a built-in sidebar — and if you've ever lost hours to a stubborn formula or a tangled client data file, this one's for you. No new app to download, no new subscription to juggle. Just plain-English instructions that get your spreadsheet work done.

What you need to know:

  • ChatGPT for Excel and Google Sheets is now available globally as an embedded sidebar — no new app required.
  • It handles trackers, budgets, formulas, multi-tab files, scenario modeling, and spreadsheet cleanup — all through natural language prompts.
  • It's available on the Free tier, meaning cost-conscious business owners can access it at zero additional cost.
  • OpenAI also launched its frontier models and Codex on AWS via Amazon Bedrock, expanding enterprise-grade AI access for cloud-native SMBs.

Business Impact

SMBs can instantly automate their most time-consuming spreadsheet tasks — from building budget trackers to cleaning up client data — using tools they already pay for, with zero additional software cost.

Your Action This Week

Open Excel or Google Sheets today and look for the ChatGPT sidebar in your toolbar. Pick one task you've been putting off — a budget template, a contact list cleanup, a formula you couldn't crack — and let it take a first pass. You might be surprised how much time you win back before Friday.

Automation & Agents

Microsoft Build 2026: AI Agents Take Center Stage

Microsoft Build 2026 kicked off this week in San Francisco, and the message for business owners was loud and clear: AI agents aren't coming — they're here. Microsoft is making a major bet that non-technical teams can now build and deploy their own AI-powered digital teammates, and they're handing SMBs the keys through Copilot Studio.

What you need to know:

  • Microsoft Build 2026 opened June 2 at Fort Mason Center in San Francisco, with agentic AI for business as its central theme.
  • Microsoft Copilot Studio is now positioned as the go-to platform for SMBs to build and deploy AI digital teammates — no coding required.
  • Microsoft unveiled 3 new proprietary AI models in its MAI series, entering into direct competition with OpenAI and Google Gemini.
  • MAI-Voice-1 can clone voices from just seconds of audio and generate speech at 60x real-time speed — opening the door to affordable voice automation for customer-facing businesses.

Business Impact

SMBs can use Microsoft Copilot Studio — already included in many Microsoft 365 plans — to automate repetitive workflows like customer intake, scheduling, and internal approvals without hiring a developer.

Your Action This Week

Check your Microsoft 365 subscription tier — Copilot Studio access may already be included. If it is, log in and explore the pre-built agent templates. Even setting up a simple FAQ bot or internal request handler can start saving your team hours each week with no developer needed.

Industry & Market

Anthropic Files for IPO at $965B Valuation

Anthropic — the company behind the Claude AI platform — confirmed its IPO filing this week following a landmark $65 billion funding round. This is a historic moment for the AI industry, and it has real implications for every business owner who's thinking about where AI fits into their operations and budget.

What you need to know:

  • Anthropic filed for its IPO around June 2, 2026, following a $65B funding round that puts its post-money valuation at approximately $965 billion.
  • Anthropic is set to become the first major AI pure-play company to go public — a genuine landmark for the industry.
  • Claude's Model Context Protocol (MCP) has become a widely adopted standard for connecting AI agents to business data and tools.
  • Agentic AI deployments are now averaging 171% ROI, with U.S. enterprises reporting returns as high as 192% — roughly 3x traditional automation.

Business Impact

Anthropic going public will intensify competition among AI providers, likely driving down API pricing and improving Claude's accessibility for SMBs building customer service bots, document automation, and sales workflows.

Your Action This Week

If you've been waiting for AI costs to come down before committing, now is a smart time to revisit your options. More competition means better pricing and more choices. Take 20 minutes this week to compare Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini for one real task in your business — drafting client emails, summarizing reports, or answering customer FAQs — and see which one fits your workflow best.
