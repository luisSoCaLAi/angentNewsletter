With 37 prebuilt sales skills ready out of the box, this is arguably the biggest CRM-AI integration of 2026. Pilot access is live right now, with an open beta dropping later this month.

  • Claude is now the default AI reasoning engine across Agentforce, Slack, and Salesforce developer tools — announced September 1, 2026
  • 37 prebuilt sales skills are ready out-of-the-box, allowing businesses to automate sales workflows without custom integrations
  • Pilot access is live now ahead of a September open beta — SMBs already on Salesforce can apply immediately

Business Impact

SMBs using Salesforce CRM can now deploy AI sales agents that read live revenue data, suggest next-best actions, and execute tasks autonomously — slashing manual CRM work and accelerating pipeline conversion.

Your Takeaway

Productivity & Operations

ChatGPT Becomes a Full Browser-Based Business Agent

OpenAI’s latest updates have turned ChatGPT into something genuinely new: a true agentic browser assistant that doesn’t just answer questions — it gets things done. With WebMCP for live web interaction, multi-account Google Workspace support, and a desktop browser extension now spanning Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi, ChatGPT is quickly becoming the always-on digital employee SMBs never knew they needed.

  • OpenAI’s WebMCP gives ChatGPT agents direct access to live websites and signed-in sessions — enabling real task execution inside business tools
  • ChatGPT now supports multiple Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts) in one conversation, merging personal and work workflows seamlessly
  • The ChatGPT desktop browser extension expanded to Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi — letting users control browser tasks and reference open tabs via AI

Business Impact

SMBs can now use ChatGPT as a hands-free operator across their browser-based tools — scheduling meetings, drafting replies, pulling data, and completing web tasks — dramatically cutting administrative overhead with no extra software.

Your Takeaway

Install the ChatGPT browser extension on your team’s primary browser this week and connect your Google Workspace accounts. Start with one repetitive task — scheduling, inbox triage, or web research — and let it run for five days. The time savings will speak for themselves.
