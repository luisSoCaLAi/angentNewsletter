Topic 3 — Trend Watch

Agentic AI Workflows Now Core to SMB Operations

“Agentic AI” has been a buzzword for a while — but this week it officially graduated to business reality. Autonomous AI agents (software that plans, decides, and executes multi-step tasks without constant hand-holding) went mainstream across platforms that SMBs actually use. McKinsey and PwC both called 2026 the tipping point where AI moves from pilot programs to full production deployment, and the numbers back it up: AI is now writing 65%+ of Snap’s code, and businesses using agents are reclaiming dozens of staff hours per week.

  • Creatify Agent launched an autonomous video ad production tool that analyzes your past ad performance and auto-generates fresh creatives for TikTok, Meta, and YouTube — no agency required.
  • Amazon rolled out AI-powered “Unmet Demand Insights” for sellers: agents that identify market gaps, generate product proposals, and forecast demand automatically.
  • PwC and McKinsey both identify 2026 as the year SMBs and enterprises alike shift from AI experimentation to enterprise-wide agentic AI deployment — the window to get ahead is right now.

Business Impact

SMBs can deploy off-the-shelf AI agents today to automate marketing content, inventory decisions, and customer workflows — reclaiming dozens of staff hours per week and competing with larger rivals at a fraction of the cost.

Sources: MarTech  •  About Amazon  •  PwC  •  IBM

Your Takeaway: Pick one repetitive task your team does every week — writing social captions, responding to routine customer emails, or pulling sales reports — and research one agentic tool built for that specific job. Creatify Agent, Zapier’s AI agents, and Amazon’s seller tools are great starting points. Deploy a 2-week pilot and count the hours saved.
